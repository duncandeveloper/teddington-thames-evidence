River Thames Evidence & Representation Programme

A structured, source-referenced case on the ecological health, water quality, public use and official monitoring of the River Thames between Teddington Weir and Richmond — and what needs to be done about it. Every figure published here carries a citation to its original source, so that residents, regulators and elected representatives can check the evidence for themselves rather than take it on trust.

What this is

This site publishes the working documents of an independent evidence programme concerning a single stretch of the tidal and freshwater Thames. It is not a campaign site and it does not ask you to accept a conclusion.

The purpose is narrow and practical: to assemble what is actually known about this reach of the river from official monitoring, published datasets and public records; to identify clearly where that evidence is thin, contested or absent; and to set out proportionate asks of the bodies that hold statutory responsibility for it. The documents below are working documents. They are versioned, dated, and revised as new information is obtained.

  • Sourced. Figures and claims are referenced to the dataset, publication or public record they came from.
  • Provisional. Material is published at working-draft stage and marked with its version and date, not held back until it is final.
  • Corrigible. Where a body identifies an error in this evidence, the correction is made and recorded.
